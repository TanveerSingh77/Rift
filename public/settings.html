Troubleshooting

“Sign in to confirm” errors: export cookies.txt from your browser in the Netscape format. On a cloud host set VIDGRAB_COOKIES to that file's path as a secret, then redeploy.

Extractor missing or out of date: yt-dlp is installed by the build step, never committed. Redeploying the service pulls the newest build.

Missing 1080p: the source video itself may not have 1080p. yt-dlp downloads the best available at or below your limit.

Data-saver mode caps at 720p: it redirects to a file YouTube already serves with audio, and the highest of those is 720p. Turn it off for a merged 1080p or 4K MP4.

No library is kept: nothing is stored on the server, so My Videos and My Music are always empty. Files land in your device's Downloads folder.

FLAC is not higher quality: the source is already compressed, so FLAC and WAV only repackage it and take more space. Choose Opus or MP3 unless a player demands another format.
